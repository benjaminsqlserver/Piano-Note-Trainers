About the song

"Every Praise" is a contemporary gospel anthem written and recorded by Hezekiah Walker, built around the declaration "Every praise is to our God" — a modern worship staple known for its driving, syncopated piano comping and its call-and-response lead vocal, both reproduced note for note in this lesson's Alto, Piano Right Hand, and Piano Left Hand parts.

Tips for using this lesson

  • Use each part's Mute checkbox to isolate just the Alto line, just the right-hand chords, or just the left-hand bass.
  • Scroll any keyboard horizontally to follow a part that moves outside the visible C0–C8 window.
  • Click any key directly to hear its pitch on its own instrument voice, independent of playback.
  • The Key selector transposes all three parts together, so the whole arrangement always stays in the same key relative to itself.
