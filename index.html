Learn every key by name

63 lessons and counting — starting with the letter names of every key (white, sharp, flat, and the full chromatic scale), building through the major, Dorian, and Phrygian scales, every chord triad and 7th chord, chord inversions, and dozens of jazz and gospel color chords along the way (6ths, 9ths, 11ths, 13ths, altered dominants, sus and add chords, quartal and Neapolitan colors, and more) — plus real hymn and gospel song trainers woven in throughout. Every lesson comes with guided playback, and the scale and chord lessons add hands-on MIDI exercises: circle-of-fourths drills, and 5 jazz plus 5 gospel chord progressions in every key. Rootless voicings, improvisation technique, and more are next on the roadmap. Tap the keyboard below to hear it in action.
